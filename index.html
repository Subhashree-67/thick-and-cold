From dahi to glass in four steps

1

Set the dahi

Fresh milk is set overnight until it turns thick and gently tangy.

2

Churn it slow

The dahi is whisked with cold water until it turns silky and light.

3

Sweeten and flavour

Sugar, cardamom, mango or rose go in, depending on the glass.

4

Crown with malai

A spoonful of cream, pistachio and saffron finish it. Serve it cold.

Good to know

Is lassi very sweet?

Classic is lightly sweet and tangy. Mango and rose are sweeter. Ask for less sugar any time.

What is malai?

It is the thick layer of cream that settles on top of boiled milk. We spoon it over every glass.

Why a kulhad?

Terracotta keeps the lassi cold and adds a faint earthy flavour. It is also fully biodegradable.

Can I make it vegan?

Lassi is made from dahi, so the classic recipe is not vegan. Ask us about plant-based dahi versions.
